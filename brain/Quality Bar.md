# Quality Bar

Nothing reaches a client until it passes:

- Every link resolves and goes where it says.
- Spelling and the client's name are correct.
- Every number traces to a note in this Brain.
- It reads in [[Brand Voice]].
- The ask, the date and the owner are unmistakable.

QA owns this check. A deliverable that fails comes back with the exact line to fix, not "needs work."

# Ideal Client

Our ICP is a Series A or B B2B SaaS company, 20–150 people, with a product that works and a founder who wants growth to look intentional.

Signals we like:
- A named head of marketing or growth (someone to partner with).
- $1M–$20M ARR.
- Selling to mid-market, not pure self-serve.

Red flags: pre-product, agency-hoppers, "just need a logo." Enrichment should score every signup against these signals. See [[Offer Ladder]] and [[Pricing]].

# Brand Voice

How Northwind writes, everywhere — proposals, emails, ads, reports.

- Plain and warm. Short sentences. No jargon, no hype words ("revolutionary", "synergy").
- Lead with the point, then the reason. Never bury the ask.
- Specific over vague: a number, a date, a name.
- One idea per paragraph. White space is a feature.

A client email opens with the answer, not "Hope you're well." A proposal opens with the outcome, never the company history. See [[Quality Bar]].

# Pricing

Standing rates for the [[Offer Ladder]]. These are the only numbers a proposal may use.

- **Sprint** — $8,000 fixed, two weeks.
- **Partner** — $6,500 / month, three-month minimum.
- **Atelier** — $16,000 / month, six-month minimum.

Discounts: up to 10% for a six-month prepay, signed off by the Sales Lead. Nothing lower without the founder.

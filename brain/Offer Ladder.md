# Offer Ladder

Three offers, always presented as a ladder. Recommend the middle one.

1. **Sprint** — a two-week fixed-scope engagement. Brand refresh, a landing page, or a lifecycle audit. Entry point for new clients.
2. **Partner** — a monthly retainer covering one line (brand, growth, or email) with a named lead. Our bread and butter.
3. **Atelier** — full-stack: all three lines, quarterly planning, priority queue. For clients scaling fast.

Prices live in [[Pricing]]. Never invent a price. Proposals follow [[Brand Voice]].

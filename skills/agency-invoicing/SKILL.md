# Invoicing and collections

Condensed from Agency Agents Bookkeeper & Controller — AR (MIT).

Use this to raise every invoice and chase overdue — politely, relentlessly.

1. Invoice from the agreed amount in the books or [[Pricing]]. Never invent a fee.
2. Clear line items, due date, and who owes. Match the client name on the deal.
3. Aging: current, 30, 60, 90+. Oldest and largest first.
4. Chase with a fact (invoice id, amount, days) and one ask. Escalate after the agreed cadence.
5. Draft only — do not send or mark paid unless the task says so.

Output: invoice draft or collections list with next action.

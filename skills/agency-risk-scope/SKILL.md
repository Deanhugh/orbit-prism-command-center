# Risk and scope

Condensed from Agency Agents Senior Project Manager (MIT).

Use this for risks, blockers, dependencies, and realistic scope.

1. Quote the spec. Do not gold-plate. If it is not in the ask, it is a change.
2. Break work into tasks a specialist can finish, each with acceptance criteria.
3. Risk register: likelihood, impact, owner, trigger, mitigation. Escalate early.
4. Dependencies named with the other desk and the date they must land.
5. When the spec is thin, list the gaps and the assumption you will proceed on until answered.

Output: task list or risk register. No luxury extras. No silent scope.

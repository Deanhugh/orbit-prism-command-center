# Social engine

Condensed from Agency Agents Social Media Strategist (MIT).

Use this for cross-channel social — hooks, reels, carousels, calendar.

1. One theme, native cuts. LinkedIn holds the long thought; X and short video get the hook.
2. Calendar first: objective, audience, cadence, owner. Then the posts.
3. Hook in the first line or first second. One CTA. Brand voice stays consistent; tone flexes by platform.
4. Thought leadership is useful commentary, not announcement spam.
5. Listen: competitors, replies, what already earned saves or shares. Recycle winners; kill leftovers.
6. Report reach, engagement, and pipeline contribution — not vanity follower counts alone.

Output: week calendar plus the copy. Studio for filmable cuts. Do not post unless the task says so.

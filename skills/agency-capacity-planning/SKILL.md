# Capacity and resourcing

Condensed from Agency Agents Sprint Prioritizer (MIT).

Use this for capacity, resourcing, and people conflicts across a portfolio.

1. Inventory who is on what, through when. One person cannot own two critical paths.
2. Prioritize by outcome and date, not by who shouted last. Make the trade explicit.
3. Conflicts: name the two projects, the shared person, and the recommendation (sequence, split, or escalate).
4. Leave load for interrupts. A 100% plan is already late.
5. Tell the Program Manager when the portfolio does not fit the headcount.

Output: capacity grid and the one staffing decision needed this week.

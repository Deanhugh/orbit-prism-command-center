# Key-account relationship

Condensed from Agency Agents Customer Success Manager — relationship (MIT).

Use this as the human touch on key accounts.

1. Know the people: names, roles, influence, sentiment, last real conversation.
2. Show up with their goal, not our agenda. Document commitments and keep them.
3. Executive cadence even when the account is green — that is when advocacy is built.
4. Never sacrifice the relationship for a rushed transaction.
5. When something breaks, tell them before they find it.

Output: relationship map and the next human conversation worth having.

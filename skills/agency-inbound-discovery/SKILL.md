# Inbound discovery

Condensed from Agency Agents Discovery Coach (MIT).

Use this to qualify inbound within the hour, log it, and book the call.

1. Homework before questions. Two or three Situation questions max.
2. SPIN: Problem, then Implication (cost of status quo), then Need-payoff in their words.
3. Map the gap: current state, future state, quantified impact. Do-nothing is a competitor.
4. Disqualify early when there is no pain, no path to budget, or no fit. A bad yes is tomorrow's churn.
5. Log the CRM the same hour: source, gap, next step, date. One CTA — the booked call.

Output: qualification note + suggested booking line. Draft only unless the task says send.

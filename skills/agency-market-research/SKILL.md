# Market research brief

Condensed from Agency Agents Trend Researcher + Research Synthesist (MIT).

Use this for any scan of the market, audience, or competitors. Produce a brief the rest of Marketing can run on.

1. Frame the question: subject, comparison, outcome. State search bounds (sources, date range, exclusions).
2. Collect signals from several kinds of source — search, social, funding, product pages — not one keyword dump.
3. Grade evidence. Trace a repeated claim to its origin. Volume of citations is not strength if they share one study.
4. Separate well-established, contested, single-study, and gaps. Calibrate confidence to the weakest necessary link.
5. Size the opportunity (TAM / SAM / SOM) only when numbers can be sourced. Otherwise say the gap.
6. Close with what changed, who it affects, and the one action Marketing should take this week.

Output: a short brief, then a source table (claim, origin, weight). Never invent a statistic.

# Content strategy

Condensed from Agency Agents Content Creator (MIT).

Use this for calendars, pillars, and any piece that must perform.

1. Start from positioning and the audience, not a blank calendar. Name 3–5 content pillars.
2. One story, many cuts: long-form first, then script, social, email, and Studio beats.
3. Open with the outcome the reader wants. Write in [[Brand Voice]]: plain, warm, specific.
4. SEO serves the piece — one primary keyword, honest intent, no stuffing.
5. Repurpose on purpose: each platform gets a native cut, not a paste.
6. Measure what the piece was for (traffic, leads, watch-through). Say the target.

Output: calendar row or finished draft plus the cuts. Hand social cuts to Social; search briefs to AEO / SEO.

# Proposal narrative

Condensed from Agency Agents Proposal Strategist (MIT). Complements proposal.

Use this with the existing proposal skill (prices from [[Pricing]], three options, Brand Voice).

1. If you can swap the client name and the pages still work, it is already losing.
2. Three to five win themes: their challenge, our proof, a measurable outcome. Weave them; do not slogan.
3. Three acts: their world, the journey, the transformed state. Features map to Act I pains.
4. Executive summary is the closing argument on page one — situation, tension, thesis, proof, outcome.
5. No empty adjectives. Every claim needs a metric, case, or method. Never attack a competitor by name.
6. Value before the number. Recommend the middle option on the Offer Ladder.

Output: win-theme matrix + the proposal body.

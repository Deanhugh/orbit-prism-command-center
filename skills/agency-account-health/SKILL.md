# Account health

Condensed from Agency Agents Customer Success Manager — health (MIT).

Use this for a book of accounts — health, check-ins, issues.

1. Score adoption, outcomes, relationship, support, commercial signals. Say why, not just the color.
2. Champion departure is a red event the same day. Re-map the account.
3. Check-ins exist to move a goal, not to “touch base.” Arrive with the number and the ask.
4. Issues: own the next step, the owner, the date. Follow through or trust dies faster than a bug.
5. Escalate product or delivery gaps with evidence, not vibe.

Output: health card per named account and the intervention this week.

# Lifecycle email

Condensed from Agency Agents Email Marketing Strategist (MIT).

Use this for campaigns, sequences, and the note subscribers actually open.

1. Who receives this? Define the segment on two or more attributes. No broadcast.
2. Lifecycle first: welcome, nurture, reactivation, win-back. A Won contact never gets a cold nurture.
3. Every sequence needs trigger, timing, exit conditions (convert, unsub, hard bounce, complaint).
4. Judge CTR, CTOR, and conversion. Open rate is directional after Apple MPP.
5. Consent is infrastructure: documented, one-click withdrawable. Transactional and marketing stay on separate senders.
6. Clean the list before volume. Hard bounces out. Draft only — do not send unless the task says so.

Output: sequence spec (trigger, segment, emails, exits, targets) or a send-ready draft.

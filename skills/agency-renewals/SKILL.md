# Renewals

Condensed from Agency Agents Customer Success Manager — renewal (MIT).

Use this for renewals and getting ahead of churn.

1. Renewal is a 90-day program, not a 30-day surprise.
2. Leading signals: declining use, sponsor or champion gone, unpaid invoices, escalation pattern.
3. Save play on red: restore value, reset stakeholders, honest product limits. Do not buy the year with a fake roadmap.
4. Multi-year only after the account is green and the outcome is documented.
5. Commercials come after the value story. Prices from [[Pricing]] — never invent one.

Output: renewal forecast (commit / risk / lose) and the save or close plan.

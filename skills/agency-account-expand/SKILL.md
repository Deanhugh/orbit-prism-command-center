# Land and expand

Condensed from Agency Agents Account Strategist (MIT).

Use this to grow accounts — expansion and upsell across the book.

1. Health first. Never pitch more into an account that has not won with what they have.
2. A signal needs context, timing, and a stakeholder who cares — or it is only an observation.
3. Whitespace: products not adopted, seats unused, departments not live. Business case from their side.
4. Multi-thread. If the champion left tomorrow, two other people should still know the value.
5. QBR is forward strategy, not a recap. Close with a mutual action plan.
6. NRR over bookings. Contraction and churn count.

Output: expansion plan (whitespace, map, play, owner).

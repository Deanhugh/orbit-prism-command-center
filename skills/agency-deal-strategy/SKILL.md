# Deal strategy

Condensed from Agency Agents Deal Strategist + Pipeline Analyst (MIT).

Use this to run the CRM pipeline, close, and warm-transfer to Account Management.

1. MEDDPICC every live deal: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Pain, Champion, Competition. Under 5 of 8 filled = underqualified.
2. Champion test: they will do something hard. A friendly contact is not a champion.
3. Competition includes do-nothing. Win / battle / lose zones — do not lie in a lose zone.
4. Forecast: Commit / Best case / Upside from velocity and engagement, not gut. Stale late-stage is dying.
5. Coverage without quality is vanity. Kill or repair zombie deals in the review.
6. Closed-won: warm-transfer notes (promise, people, success criteria) to Account Management the same day.

Output: pipeline review with one intervention per at-risk deal.

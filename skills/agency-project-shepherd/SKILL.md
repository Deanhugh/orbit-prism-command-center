# Project shepherd

Condensed from Agency Agents Project Shepherd (MIT).

Use this for scope, plan, milestones, and on-time delivery of client work.

1. Charter: problem, objectives, in / out of scope, success criteria, sponsor.
2. Map stakeholders and the communication cadence. Honest status even when the news is bad.
3. Critical path and dependencies first. Buffer for the unknown; never promise a fantasy date.
4. Change control: new ask = impact on date, cost, or scope, written, before yes.
5. Escalate with a recommended fix, not a problem dump.
6. Close with lessons and what Account Management should know at handoff.

Output: charter or weekly status (done / next / risk). Use Plane titles from the Brain when they exist.

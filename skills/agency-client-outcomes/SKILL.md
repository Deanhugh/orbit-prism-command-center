# Client outcomes

Condensed from Agency Agents Analytics Reporter (MIT). Complements client-report.

Use this to show adoption and outcomes so the account sees real results.

1. Every number traces to the Brain or a named source. Missing metric: say so.
2. Lead with the result. Then: what we did, what it moved, what's next.
3. Confidence on the claim. Do not launder disagreement into a clean story.
4. Flag risks in plain language — no surprises at renewal.
5. Pass [[Quality Bar]] before it goes to the client.

Output: the monthly shape in client-report, with a source line under each figure.

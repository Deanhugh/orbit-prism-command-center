# Client onboarding

Condensed from Agency Agents Customer Success Manager — onboarding (MIT).

Use this for the warm transfer from Sales and the first 90 days.

1. Pull the deal notes: what was sold, success criteria, stakeholders, go-live.
2. Time-to-value is the metric. First win on the calendar in week one if possible.
3. Name day-to-day, champion, and executive sponsor. Book the kickoff.
4. Implementation checklist with owners and dates. Document every commitment.
5. Do not promise roadmap to save a shaky start. Honesty here is retention later.
6. Hand a clean account record to the ongoing AM when onboard is done.

Output: 30-60-90 plan and the kickoff agenda.

# Brand system

Condensed from Agency Agents Brand Guardian (MIT).

Use this for voice, look, and any asset that must stay one identity.

1. Foundation before tactics: purpose, vision, mission, values, personality, promise.
2. Visual system: primary / secondary / accent, type, logo variants, spacing. Every element works as a set.
3. Voice: three traits, do / don't lines, and example sentences in [[Brand Voice]].
4. Audit touchpoints. Flag drift. Correct with a specific swap, not “make it on brand.”
5. Protect first, then evolve. A refresh needs a reason (market, audience, offer) — not novelty.
6. Cultural fit and accessibility are part of the system, not afterthoughts.

Output: a one-page foundation, or a punch-list of violations with the replacement. Do not invent a new logo or palette unless asked.
